Type Japanese. Feel at home.

✓

Your writing stays on this device.

Enoki never uploads your text, keystrokes, or emoji searches. Only clicking “Search Google” sends your editor text to Google.

No analytics. No saved drafts or conversion history. App files and dictionaries are downloaded to get ready; your writing is never included in those requests.

A few familiar keys. A whole new language.

Type romaji (Japanese sounds in Latin letters), such as nihongo → にほんご. Press Space to choose kanji: 日本語.

Space Convert to kanji / next candidate

Enter Confirm (then press again for a new line)

↑ ↓ Choose a candidate

← → Move between phrase segments

Shift + ← → Change the segment length

Esc Revert conversion / cancel input

F6 / F7 Confirm as hiragana / katakana

Ctrl + Z Undo

1 – 9 Choose a numbered candidate

Ctrl + Space Switch Japanese / English input

The Japanese input button switches both text fields to English mode. Use English mode for emoji keywords such as “cat”, or stay in Japanese mode and type “neko” → ねこ. Click an emoji to insert it at the last cursor position in your text.
